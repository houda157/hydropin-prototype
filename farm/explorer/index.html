Farm Leak Explorer

A computer copy of a 50-hectare drip farm: a pump, a 600 m main pipe and 6 fields, two watered at a time. We opened 2,070 pretend leaks on it and recorded what pressure sensors would read. Pick a leak to see its "fingerprint", or place sensors and see how many leaks they would catch.

"Right field" uses a simple rule: the sensor that drops most beyond the others' average points to its field (or the main pipe). Role 2's real placement tool optimises this properly (greedy + genetic search) and re-tests the choice on 10 farm versions it never saw. Cost: 217 DT per sensor node + 75 DT per buried tapping point (master document prices).

Data: farm/leaks/out on branch feat/farm-model (2,070 scenarios = 3 shifts × 10 farm versions × 23 leak spots × 3 sizes). Pressure changes are noise-free; the 3σ rule adds the noise. All values are simulated. Farm layout, pipe sizes and the pump are assumptions listed in farm/model/params.yaml.
